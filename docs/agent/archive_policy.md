# Archive Policy

Keep active instructions in:

- `AGENTS.md`
- `Plugins/llplayer-codex/skills/**/SKILL.md`
- `.codex/agents/*.toml`
- `docs/agent/*.md`
- `scripts/codex/*.ps1`

Legacy files such as `CLAUDE.md` and `GEMINI.md` must stay short pointers to `AGENTS.md`.

When retiring an instruction, remove or replace it. Do not leave duplicate active rules that can drift.
